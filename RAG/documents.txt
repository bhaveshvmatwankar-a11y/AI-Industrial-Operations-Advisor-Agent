CNC MACHINE MAINTENANCE

High vibration in a CNC machine can indicate tool imbalance, loose components, worn bearings, improper alignment, or excessive cutting speed.

---DOCUMENT_SEPARATOR---

If a CNC machine shows abnormal vibration, the maintenance team should inspect the cutting tools, tool holders, machine alignment, bearings, and moving components.

Continuous operation with high vibration can accelerate component wear and may lead to unexpected machine failure.

---DOCUMENT_SEPARATOR---

HYDRAULIC PRESS MAINTENANCE

High temperature in a hydraulic press may indicate low hydraulic fluid, excessive friction, pump problems, blocked filters, or operation under excessive load.

---DOCUMENT_SEPARATOR---

The maintenance team should check the hydraulic oil level, hydraulic pump, filters, cooling system, and possible fluid leaks.

If abnormal temperature continues, the machine should be inspected and preventive maintenance should be scheduled.

GENERATOR MAINTENANCE

High temperature in a generator may be caused by excessive load, insufficient cooling, low lubricant levels, blocked air filters, or engine problems.

High vibration in a generator may indicate imbalance, misalignment, loose components, or bearing damage.

---DOCUMENT_SEPARATOR---

Maintenance personnel should inspect the cooling system, lubrication system, bearings, mounting, and electrical connections.

GENERAL MACHINE HEALTH CONDITIONS

A CRITICAL machine condition indicates a high risk of failure or unsafe operation. Critical machines require immediate inspection by qualified maintenance personnel.

---DOCUMENT_SEPARATOR---

A WARNING machine condition indicates abnormal behavior that requires monitoring and proactive maintenance before the condition becomes critical.

A HEALTHY machine is operating within normal parameters. Healthy machines should continue with their scheduled preventive maintenance.

---DOCUMENT_SEPARATOR---

MAINTENANCE PRIORITY

Critical machines have the highest maintenance priority and should be inspected immediately.

Warning machines should be monitored more frequently and should receive planned preventive maintenance.

---DOCUMENT_SEPARATOR---

Healthy machines should continue normal operation and regular preventive maintenance.
